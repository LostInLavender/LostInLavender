Evelyn wakes up one morning with a heavy mind,
carrying thoughts she can’t quite explain. Hoping to clear her head,
she goes for a walk, drifting through unfamiliar lands
until she notices something strange in the distance a massive gate
she has never seen before.
Curiosity pulls her closer. When she steps through it,
she discovers an endless lavender field stretching beyond
the horizon. It feels strangely familiar, almost like home,
even though she knows she’s completely lost.
The silence, the color, the stillness
everything feels like it understands her in a way nothing else does.
The scent of lavender is intoxicating.
